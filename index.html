03 Calculation Logic
F1
Raw material cost per tonne (£)
Raw material cost = ( Paper price × 985.92 ) + ( Alginate price × 14.08 )
F2
Logistics cost per tonne (£)
Logistics cost = ( Paper distance × 0.0394368 ) + ( Alginate distance × 0.0004224 )
F3
Conversion cost per tonne (£)
Conversion cost = Coating converter price × 1000
F4
Total cost per tonne (£)
Total cost per tonne = Raw material cost + Logistics cost + Conversion cost
F5
Total cost per m² (£)
Total cost per m² = Total cost per tonne × 0.000284
